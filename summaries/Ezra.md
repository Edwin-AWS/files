# Ezra

## Introduction

🆕 Book: Ezra

Introducing Ezra
God's faithfulness in the ruins stands.

Ezra is not just a record of returning exiles. It's a quiet anthem of how God rebuilds His people when they seem forgotten — through kings, scribes, and weeping prayers in the dust. It chronicles God's faithfulness and His grace for a nation in dispersion and in ruins.

✍🏽 Authorship
#️⃣ Undoubtedly, Ezra the scribe is the voice behind the book. The meticulous detail, familiarity with Persian policies, similarity of structure with its twin book of Nehemiah, and the shift from third person in chapters 1–6 to first person in chapters 7–10 attest to his personal hand in the second half. The detailed genealogies, temple lists, and official correspondence to statesmen all point to one man: Ezra.

👳🏻‍♂️ The Man — Ezra
#️⃣ Josephus describes him as a man of learning and courage who won the respect of Artaxerxes.
⏩ He was a man of opportunity, raised by God to lead a spiritual reformation (Prophets and Kings, chapters 45–47).
⏩ Others called him the theologian of reformation. And to sum it up, Scripture says he was a man who had set his heart to study the law of God, to live it, and to teach it (Ezra 7:25).

📖 The Book and Key Themes
#️⃣ A closer look at the book reveals God's sovereignty, redemption, opposition and war, reformation and obedience, identity, separation, and devotion to the covenant of Yahweh.

✍🏽 Key Verses
#️⃣ Ezra 1:1 — "The Lord stirred up the spirit of Cyrus." God moves hearts beyond the visible church.
#️⃣ Ezra 3:11–13 — Joy and weeping mingle at the foundation. Restoration is never just nostalgia; it's hope with tears.
#️⃣ Ezra 7:10 — The model of discipleship: know, live, teach.
#️⃣ Ezra 9:6 — "I am ashamed and blush to lift my face." True leadership owns corporate sin.
#️⃣ Ezra 10:4 — "Arise, for it is your task, and we are with you."

🏹 Thrust
#️⃣ The focus is on contemporary application of the historical narrative, with a major emphasis on drawing out the key themes: tracing God's sovereignty, and its relevance to eschatology, ecclesiology, and practical application.

🛐 Prayer
Lord, we ask for the presence of Your Holy Spirit as we study the book of Ezra. Give us an understanding of Your word. Amen.

## Ezra 1 — Cyrus's Decree to Rebuild

### Summary

Cyrus, king of Persia, issues a decree allowing the Jewish exiles to return to Jerusalem and rebuild the temple, moved by God to fulfill His promise of restoration.

### Key Lessons

**God's Empire**

1️⃣ God's Empire

#️⃣ God is the supreme ruler; in fact, God moves empires. He has power even over heathen kings and kingdoms.
#️⃣ God can use any move to advance His purpose. By stirring the heart of the heathen king, it becomes clear that God has His servants even in places we would humanly rule out.
⏩ However, we cannot baptize every political agenda on the pretext that God is in it. Not every political move is from God, but God can use any move (Ezra 1:1; 8:22).
⏩ God keeps His promises; He moves His clock in His time. The turn of events only moves in fulfillment of His word (Jeremiah 25:11–12; 29:10).

**The Temple**

2️⃣ The Temple

#️⃣ The temple is central to the reformation and restoration of Judah as a people.
#️⃣ Cyrus uses imperial power for temple restoration. It is interesting to note that:
(i) in the wilderness, the tabernacle's provisions came from Egypt;
(ii) for Ezra's temple, they came from Cyrus, a heathen; and later
(iii) Herod will provide for the temple in Jesus' time.
The silver, even in the hands of the king or the state, belongs to God too (Psalm 24:1).

**Eschatology Lens**

3️⃣ Eschatology Lens

#️⃣ The fulfillment of the seventy years is a partial fulfillment of the bigger prophecies still ahead. Just as God was faithful in keeping His promise (Jeremiah 25:11–12; 29:10), so in due time He will also fulfill all the end-time prophecies.

**The Church**

4️⃣ The Church

#️⃣ God, in His grace, is always the first mover in the plan of restoration.
⏩ Membership is not automatic by culture or birth; it's by allegiance. Only "those whose spirit God had stirred" went up (v. 5).
⏩ To be part of God's family is a voluntary choice (John 1:12; 3:16). Those who accept God's call and move at His bidding are indeed His children (John 15:4).

> 🙏 **Prayer:** Lord, help us to have hearts that respond when You call us to action. Amen.

## Ezra 2 — The Census of the Returning Exiles

### Summary

Ezra 2 records the census of the exiles who returned with Zerubbabel — families, priests, Levites, and temple servants — identified by name and lineage as they resettled in their towns.

### Key Lessons

**God's Empire**

1️⃣ God's Empire

#️⃣ God's empire has subjects who are identified. Just as the returnees were identified as families, priests, Levites, and servants, so God too has such under His banner.
⏩ God cares about names, lineages, and qualifications.
⏩ God pays attention to details, because details protect identity.

**The Temple**

2️⃣ The Temple

#️⃣ We are God's temple when we identify with God's remnant.

**Eschatology Lens**

3️⃣ Eschatology Lens

#️⃣ God preserves a people for His end-time purpose. The list is a remnant list.
⏩ In a globalized world, God's people must be set apart, holy, and identifiable.
⏩ God's church shall consist of a remnant who will be true to God's covenant, His commandments, and His testimony.

**Ecclesiology Lens**

4️⃣ Ecclesiology Lens

#️⃣ Priests without a genealogy are excluded (vv. 62–63). Such boundaries are not racism; they are covenant protection.

> 🙏 **Prayer:** Lord, help us to be identifiable as Yours by our conduct, faith, and testimony. Amen.

## Ezra 3 — The Altar and Temple Foundation Restored

### Summary

The altar is rebuilt and the temple's foundation is laid, drawing a mixed response of weeping from those who remembered the former temple's glory and shouts of joy from those celebrating the new beginning.

### Key Lessons

**God's Kingdom**

1️⃣ God's Kingdom

#️⃣ Check your priorities — worship always precedes building in God's kingdom.
⏩ Reformation in God's kingdom begins with honoring God, not impressing people. Where the kingdom of God reigns, the altar, as a symbol of worship, must be the banner.

**The Temple through an Eschatological Lens**

2️⃣ The Temple through an Eschatological Lens

#️⃣ Studying the significance of the temple in the context of Haggai 2:9 offers great nuance and lessons.
⏩ According to Haggai, "the glory of this latter house will be greater" (Haggai 2:9).
#️⃣ A closer comparison of Solomon's Temple, Ezra's temple, and Herod's temple shows the following:
(i) in terms of physical beauty and size, Solomon's was greater;
(ii) in terms of the visible glory of God descending, there is no record of it in the second temple;
(iii) in terms of materials, Solomon's temple used superior materials.
⏩ The weeping, then, was justified for what had been lost.
#️⃣ The shouting, seen through a prophetic lens, pointed to what was coming — a temple far greater even than Herod's, in the person of Jesus. For in Christ, God Himself descended in human form (John 1).
⏩ The builders of Solomon's temple were sinners, and those of Ezra's and Herod's temples were funded by heathens. Yet in Christ, God Himself is the builder.
⏩ The sound of joy in our time must be even greater, read in the context of Revelation 21, where God Himself will tabernacle among His people.

**Ecclesiology Lens**

3️⃣ Ecclesiology Lens

#️⃣ God's people will be identified by corporate worship and corporate community (Hebrews 10:25).
⏩ There must be an altar. The worship of God must be the foundation and center of all worship.
⏩ God is building Himself a church in whose hearts He is going to dwell. He will be manifested in their characters before He finally dwells among them in eternity.

> 🙏 **Prayer:** Lord, as we build physical churches, may You build us spiritually into Your likeness. Amen.

## Ezra 4 — Opposition Halts the Work

### Summary

Local opponents oppose the rebuilding work, using letters and accusations to pressure the Persian king Artaxerxes into halting construction, and the work stalls under this opposition.

### Key Lessons

**God's Empire**

1️⃣ God's Empire

#️⃣ Read in the context of the great controversy, God's kingdom has opponents (Revelation 12).
⏩ Just as the accusations in Ezra 4:12–13 were not true, God's opponents through the ages have weaponized half-truths in fighting God's kingdom.
⏩ In the past, many Christians were persecuted using the trump card of fear — the claim that they were a threat to the political and economic well-being of the earthly empire (Hebrews 11).

**Eschatology Lens**

2️⃣ Eschatology Lens

#️⃣ Opposition is, and will still be, part of God's people's experience. The kingdom advances through resistance, and God's people must never expect smooth sailing.
⏩ God's presence among His people must never be mistaken for immunity to opposition, temptation, and trials. In the context of Revelation 12:17, the Christian walk is one of warfare.
⏩ Just as an alliance of power, influence, and legal and political machinery was used in Ezra's time, the same forces are echoed in the book of Revelation among the tools of final persecution.

**Ecclesiology Lens**

3️⃣ Ecclesiology Lens

#️⃣ Wisdom knows when to pause without surrendering identity. There are times when wisdom dictates a pause in the face of temporary limits (v. 21).
⏩ Not everyone who offers to be an ally of God qualifies to be part of the team (vv. 1–4). Some enemies come masquerading as supporters of the mission, yet their real intention is to derail it through infiltration.
⏩ An enemy within is harder to fight than an enemy outside.

> 🙏 **Prayer:** Lord, give us the power to advance Your kingdom. Where the adversary seems to overpower us, help us to cling to You all the more. Amen.

## Ezra 5 — The Work Resumes

### Summary

Encouraged by the prophets Haggai and Zechariah, Zerubbabel and Jeshua resume the temple work despite lacking official approval, and the Persian governor Tattenai writes to King Darius to verify their authority.

### Key Lessons

**The Builder Needs the Preacher**

1️⃣ The Builder Needs the Preacher (v. 1)

#️⃣ There is a common delusion that preaching will never be attended by practical results. In fact, the builder and the preacher are partners.
⏩ The counsel of 2 Chronicles 20:20 applies: believe in the Lord your God and you will be established; believe His prophets and you will prosper.
⏩ The church does not need a new mission statement; it simply needs to be pointed back to its reason for existing.
⏩ It is the preacher's work to point back to God's standard as His only way for His mission.

**God Always Sends a Word in Due Season**

2️⃣ God Always Sends a Word in Due Season

#️⃣ Discouragement must be met with God's word of encouragement.
⏩ Haggai and Zechariah give the word for the moment.
✍🏽 "How beautiful upon the mountains are the feet of him that bringeth good tidings, and that preacheth peace: of him that sheweth forth good, that preacheth salvation, that saith unto Zion, Thy God reigneth!" (Isaiah 52:7)

**God's Church Needs a Zerubbabel**

3️⃣ God's Church Needs a Zerubbabel (v. 2)

#️⃣ The church needs leaders who will stand up to the challenge — men who will put their hand to the plough, men of influence and power.
⏩ Men whose mantra is "let us do God's work" — who will not only preach but lead the work appropriately.
⏩ Men of dignity and power, who will employ their means and influence to give life to good work.

**God Turns Opposition and Resistance in Favor of His People**

4️⃣ God Turns Opposition and Resistance in Favor of His People (v. 5)

#️⃣ History is not moving aimlessly; God has His eye upon His church, and ultimately His purpose will be served.

**Faith Waits for No Protocol**

5️⃣ Faith Waits for No Protocol

#️⃣ Start again in faith, even without protocol or approval. They began to build (v. 2) before approval came.
⏩ We are to recognize human government as an ordinance of divine appointment... we are to obey the laws, except when they conflict with the law of God (Ellen G. White, Acts of the Apostles, p. 69).

**Your Testimony Must Be Your Defense**

6️⃣ Your Testimony Must Be Your Defense (vv. 11–16)

#️⃣ Tell your story of how God has been faithful to you. This is the good news — that He still works even among His people today.

> 🙏 **Prayer:** Lord, help me to be principled and a person of influence for the advancement of Your work. Amen.

## Ezra 6 — The Temple Is Completed

### Summary

King Darius finds Cyrus's original decree, orders the opposition to cease and to fund the temple's completion, and the Jews finish and dedicate the temple, celebrating the Passover with joy.

### Key Lessons

**God's Sovereignty**

1️⃣ God's Sovereignty

#️⃣ God preserves history to advance His cause.
⏩ God moves kings, even to hasten His work (v. 12).
⏩ God has already made provision ahead of time (vv. 8–9).
⏩ God protects His purpose and His church (v. 11).
⏩ God's reign brings joy (v. 22).
⏩ Subjects of God's grace will always have a song of rejoicing in their hearts. Amazing grace will always be the song of gratitude on their lips, for the story of redemption and restoration is in Christ.

**The Temple**

2️⃣ The Temple

⏩ Let the church live again.
⏩ Give back what belongs to God's church.
⏩ Stop opposing God's mission (v. 7).
⏩ God has provision for His purpose, even from sources we least expect (vv. 8–9).

**Eschatological Lens**

3️⃣ Eschatological Lens

#️⃣ The work of restoring and rebuilding the characters of God's people must be the preoccupation of the end time.
⏩ Christ is waiting for His character to be replicated in His people, until we all reach the stature and measure of the standard of the man in Christ Jesus.
⏩ Just as the worship ceremonies were reinstituted after the temple's completion, God must regain His reign in our hearts, whose temples we are.

**The Church**

4️⃣ The Church

#️⃣ Rebuilding God's church means rebuilding societies and families.
⏩ The text says they prospered through the prophesying of Haggai (v. 14). When God's people set themselves apart, grounded in the standards of righteousness, prophecy, and incorruptible purity, even the standards of human living will be uplifted.
⏩ We have not gained the favor of our current empires as Ezra's generation did (v. 22), because the church has left her post. When people turn back to God, even nations will prosper.

> 🙏 **Prayer:** Our Father in heaven, may our characters be in the likeness of Christ Jesus, so we may be fit for heaven. Amen.

## Ezra 7 — Ezra Comes to Jerusalem

### Summary

Ezra himself now enters the narrative, leading a second wave of exiles back to Jerusalem, having set his heart to study the law of the Lord, to practice it, and to teach it in Israel.

### Key Lessons

**God's Sovereignty**

1️⃣ God's Sovereignty

#️⃣ God's empire is built on a solid foundation of men who stand up to His calling and who will hold the fort in their calling.
⏩ Ezra's lineage is rich; the names in his line can never be discounted in their standing before God.
⏩ God's work of restoration is progressive. A dedicated physical structure needed dedicated hearts anchored on a renewed covenant.
⏩ Subjects of God's empire are ever on an upward, progressive journey — moving from grace to grace, from faith to faith, and finally from one level of glory to another.
✍🏽 Speaking of eternity, Ellen White says: "Every power will be developed, every capability increased. The grandest enterprises will be carried forward, the loftiest aspirations will be reached, the highest ambitions realized. And still there will appear new heights to surmount, new wonders to admire, new truths to comprehend, fresh objects of study to call forth the powers of body and mind and soul." (Ellen G. White, Heaven, p. 186.1)
⏩ God has servants and provisions from places we least expect. He has the power to put it in the hearts of earthly kings to beautify His church (v. 27).
⏩ All resources, people included, are His (Psalm 24:1). He counts His cattle by the hills, not by number, and He counts the beasts of the forests as His also (Psalm 50:10).

**The Temple**

2️⃣ The Temple

#️⃣ Men who build God's temple must follow Ezra's model (v. 10):
⏩ Their hearts must be set apart.
⏩ They must be men of the Book, deeply immersed in searching out the riches of the mysteries and wonder-works of grace, the science of redemption, the science of true education and righteousness by faith, and the work of salvation and restoration.
⏩ They must live the word and teach it.

**Eschatology Lens**

3️⃣ Eschatology Lens

#️⃣ God prepares leaders for His kingdom.
⏩ Ezra 7 shows God raising up a leader skilled in the law to lead His people. God still prepares leaders for His harvest.
⏩ Just as John was the forerunner of the Messiah, God promises to send men in the spirit of Elijah (Malachi 4:4–6) before the great day. Like Ezra, such a one will turn hearts to the law.
⏩ God's remnant has a special message, summarized in the three angels' messages of Revelation 14.
⏩ Just as God used Ezra to restore His law, He will use Spirit-empowered leaders to prepare the way for Christ's return.

> 🙏 **Prayer:** Lord, may our hearts be turned to You, so we live in the fear of You daily. Amen.

## Ezra 8 — Ezra Prepares for the Journey

### Summary

Ezra 8 profiles Ezra's preparations for the journey to Jerusalem — noticing the absence of Levites, calling a fast instead of requesting a military escort, and safely delivering the people and the temple treasures.

### Key Lessons

**Leaders Must Have Sanctified Common Sense**

1️⃣ Leaders Must Have Sanctified Common Sense (vv. 15–20)

#️⃣ Ezra notices that no Levites had come. He stops, sends for them, discerns the problem, and recruits thirty-eight men plus two hundred and twenty temple servants.
⏩ Leaders must pay skilled attention: "I viewed the people... but found there none of the sons of Levi" (Ezra 8:15). Wisdom starts with seeing reality, not making assumptions.

**Call on God before Man**

2️⃣ Call on God before Man

#️⃣ Ezra (vv. 21–23) calls a fast instead of asking the king for soldiers.
⏩ He became a mouthpiece for God... he made the study of the Scriptures the preparation for his life's work (Ellen G. White, Prophets and Kings, p. 609).
✍🏽 "Those who stand in positions of trust should be men of keen foresight... men who will work from principle, not impulse." (Ellen G. White, Gospel Workers, p. 95)
⏩ Leaders must act on principle, not impulse. Instead of asking for Persian troops (impulse), he acted on principle, in fasting and prayer. He never rushed, but weighed his options for three days at the river Ahava (v. 15). That is sanctified common sense, not anxious control.

**Trust God's Hand and Faithful Presence**

3️⃣ Trust God's Hand and Faithful Presence (v. 22)

#️⃣ "I was ashamed to require of the king a band of soldiers... for we had spoken unto the king, saying, The hand of our God is upon all them for good."
⏩ God's testimony must count for more than protection from earthly kings. This chapter also teaches us how we must deal with kings and governments.
✍🏽 "We are to recognize human government... We are to obey the laws, except when they conflict with the law of God." (Ellen G. White, Acts of the Apostles, p. 69)
✍🏽 "The church has not been established as a political power... Her mission is to proclaim the gospel." (Ellen G. White, Gospel Workers, p. 391)
✍🏽 "Use the resources of the world's kingdoms, but don't be owned by them. God's people are to be separate... but not in a narrow, exclusive spirit." (Ellen G. White, Testimonies, vol. 1, p. 356)
✍🏽 "The church is God's appointed agency for the salvation of men. It was organized for service." (Ellen G. White, Acts of the Apostles, p. 9)

> 🙏 **Prayer:** Lord, help us to act from principle. Remind us of Your mission, that we may act out of purpose rather than earthly power. Amen.

## Ezra 9 — Ezra's Prayer of Confession

### Summary

Leaders report to Ezra that Israelites, priests, and Levites have widely intermarried with neighboring pagan nations, and Ezra responds with grief, tearing his garments and offering a prayer of confession on behalf of the nation.

### Key Lessons

**The Root Cause of All Apostasy Is Compromise**

1️⃣ The Root Cause of All Apostasy Is Compromise (vv. 1–2)

#️⃣ When God's people prioritize unity over purity, they compromise their witness.
⏩ Cultural assimilation without discernment leads to spiritual compromise.
✍🏽 "Grief over sin is the first step toward genuine repentance. True confession requires genuine regret, humility, and taking personal responsibility." (Ellen G. White, Testimonies, vol. 5, p. 634)

**God's Remnant**

2️⃣ God's Remnant

✍🏽 "God's people are to be a remnant, separate from the world, yet shining as lights in darkness." (Ellen G. White, The Great Controversy, p. 609)
⏩ God's remnant is not defined by numbers, but by faithfulness.
⏩ When we forget God's past mercies, we risk repeating our mistakes.
⏩ A remnant mindset prioritizes God's kingdom over cultural acceptance.

**The Church in the World**

3️⃣ The Church in the World

✍🏽 "The church must not conform to the world, but transform it through God's power." (Ellen G. White, The Desire of Ages, p. 142)
⏩ When the world says "fit in," God says "stand out."
⏩ Cultural pressure can lead to spiritual compromise, but God's Word is our anchor.
⏩ True freedom comes from obeying God's commands, not from following cultural trends.

**Ezra's Prayer**

4️⃣ Ezra's Prayer (vv. 2, 10–12)

#️⃣ Ezra's prayer and actions focus on restoring the community's purity, ensuring Israel remains distinct and holy.
⏩ This separation enables their mission to glorify God among the nations.
✍🏽 "The church is God's instrument for spreading the gospel, and its unity is crucial for its mission." (Ellen G. White, Acts of the Apostles, p. 164)
⏩ Community purity is not about exclusion, but about mission.
⏩ God's people are called to be holy, not just happy.
⏩ Unity in diversity is possible when we prioritize God's Word.

> 🙏 **Prayer:** Lord, remove us from any entanglement with sin. Create in us a new heart, and set us apart for mission. In Jesus' name we pray, Amen.

## Ezra 10 — The People Repent

### Summary

Israel responds to the call to repentance by acknowledging its sin and taking practical steps to address it — leaders are appointed, cases are investigated, and a plan is carried out to restore the community's purity.

### Key Lessons

**Repentance**

1️⃣ Repentance

✍🏽 "True repentance requires a change of heart and a commitment to obey God's Word." (Ellen G. White, Steps to Christ, p. 23)
⏩ True repentance must be followed by action (2 Corinthians 5:17).
⏩ True repentance involves making hard choices for the sake of purity.
⏩ Practical obedience is painful — sending away those cherished in the heart is no small matter. Genuine repentance comes at a cost; there are relationships to be cut off.

**Eschatology Lens**

2️⃣ Eschatology Lens (vv. 13–14)

#️⃣ Israel's swift action foreshadows an eschatological urgency, recognizing the consequences of their sin and the need for immediate repentance.
✍🏽 "The time for repentance is now; delay may mean eternal loss." (Ellen G. White, The Great Controversy, p. 609)
⏩ Never procrastinate — today is the day of salvation; tomorrow may be too late.
⏩ God's people must respond urgently to His call to repentance.
⏩ The consequences of sin are real, but God's mercy is greater.

**Counter-Cultural Choices**

3️⃣ Counter-Cultural Choices (v. 11)

#️⃣ Israel's decision to separate from foreign wives and children goes against cultural norms, demonstrating a counter-cultural commitment to God's commands.
✍🏽 "God's people must not conform to the world, but transform it through God's power." (Ellen G. White, The Desire of Ages, p. 142)
⏩ God's way is often the opposite of the world's way.
⏩ True freedom comes from obeying God's commands, not from following cultural trends.
⏩ Counter-cultural choices require courage and conviction.

**Ecclesiology Lens**

4️⃣ Ecclesiology Lens

#️⃣ God's end-time church is not called to ethnic separation, but to covenantal separation.
⏩ Restoring identity means addressing the sin problem, and restoration is possible when we work together in humility and love.

> 🙏 **Prayer:** Lord, lead us to the climax of purity, a separation from sin and defilement. Please make us purely Thine. In Jesus' name we pray, Amen.
